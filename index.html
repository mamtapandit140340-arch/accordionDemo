Are most internet users actually human?
No. Roughly 61% of all web traffic is generated by non-humans. This invisible digital ecosystem consists of automated bots, search engine indexers, scraping tools, and hacking software constantly running in the background.
What animal is featured on the Firefox browser logo?
Despite the literal name, the animal wrapped around the globe is not a fox—it is a red panda. "Firefox" is actually an English translation of a Chinese nickname for the red panda.
